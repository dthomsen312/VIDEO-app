# AI Automation Agency Pricing (2025–2026, USD)

Source-quality legend used throughout:
- **[Verified-vendor]**: the vendor's own pricing page, fetched Sept 2026.
- **[Agency/vendor blog]**: an agency or SaaS content-marketing article. These publish ranges with no survey method and have a commercial reason to anchor high or low. Treat them as indicative only.
- **[Marketplace]**: Upwork, Fiverr, Contra or Toptal pages, or aggregators describing them.
- **[Anecdotal]**: single-operator stories and community claims. reddit.com could not be reached directly: the search tool refused the domain. No Reddit thread is quoted first-hand anywhere in these notes.

Note: no independent survey of AI-automation-agency pricing was found. Every build and retainer range below comes from self-published content marketing. The ranges agree loosely but cover a wide spread. The biggest divide is between offshore or freelance SMB work and US mid-market consultancies.

## 1. Typical project fees for common builds

### Takeaway
Prices fall into three tiers that differ roughly tenfold at each step:
- **Freelance or marketplace work:** $100–$1,500 per build.
- **SMB-focused agencies:** $1,500–$15,000 per build.
- **Mid-market or enterprise consultancies:** $10k–$100k+ per build.

Two new cofounders selling to SMBs should realistically expect $1.5k–$10k per build. Voice and chat agents are most often sold as a small setup fee plus a monthly charge rather than as a large one-off fee.

### Cited Findings
**General workflow automation (CRM + Zapier/Make/n8n)**
- Simple builds of 1–2 workflows cost ~$500–$1,500. A connected stack of 3–5 workflows costs ~$1,500–$4,000. Full multi-system builds cost $10k+. Typical setup fees run $2,000–$12,000. [Agency/vendor blog] — [Taskip](https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-pricing/) (via search summary)
- Workflow automation with n8n, Make or Zapier plus an LLM costs $3,000–$15,000 one-time for 1–5 processes. [Agency/vendor blog, dated Aug 19 2026, no external data cited] — [Lets Viz](https://lets-viz.com/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-what-buyers-pay)
- A single end-to-end workflow across 3–6 tools costs $5k–$15k over 2–6 weeks. A suite of 3–6 connected workflows costs $15k–$30k. A department-wide programme costs $30k–$50k+. [Agency blog; says it draws on its own delivery records since 2019] — [The Crunch](https://thecrunch.io/ai-automation-agency-cost/)
- Upmarket agency pricing: single process $10k–$30k, multi-process $30k–$80k, enterprise platform $80k–$250k+. The same source says premium agencies charge 2–3x these rates, and new agencies may need to price at 60–80% of them to build case studies. [Agency blog, Jan 2026] — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)
- On Fiverr, a "complete workflow" in AI automation is listed at $45–$520 in 2026. [Marketplace, via aggregator] — [Layer3 Labs / search summary](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/is-an-ai-automation-agency-worth-it)
- Contra examples: an n8n workflow with AI built in costs from $2,500, and an "autonomous agent for one business workflow" costs $7,500. [Marketplace listings] — [Contra n8n freelancers](https://contra.com/hire/n8n-freelancers)

**AI chatbots / customer-support agents**
- Upmarket agency pricing: an FAQ or knowledge-base bot costs $10k–$35k, a customer-support bot $35k–$75k, and enterprise conversational AI $75k–$500k+. [Agency blog] — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)
- Most SMBs spend $1,500–$15,000 on a chatbot build. A SaaS chatbot for a small business costs $0–$200/mo on a flat plan. [Dev-shop blogs, via search summary] — [BrainX](https://brainxtech.com/blog/ai-chatbot-development-cost/); [Heeya](https://heeya.fr/en/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-chatbot-cost-2026)

**Internal RAG knowledge assistants**
- A small RAG chatbot over 10–50 documents costs $8k–$18k delivered offshore. It ships in 2–3 weeks and runs at $80–$200/mo. [Dev-shop blog] — [Imagic Solutions](https://imagicsolutions.in/blog/rag-chatbot-cost-breakdown)
- A mid-complexity custom LLM + RAG bot with CRM integration and analytics has a median cost of $75k–$120k over 8–14 weeks. A 5,000-document RAG bot at 10k conversations/mo costs $400–$1,200/mo to run. [Dev-shop blogs, via search summary] — [Kellton](https://www.kellton.com/kellton-tech-blog/custom-ai-chatbot-development-llm-rag); [ProjectCostEstimator](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/chatbot-development-cost-2026)
- RAG implementation costs $35k–$100k+, and enterprise multimodal RAG can exceed $250k. [Agency blog] — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)

**Custom agents**
- Custom agent development with LangChain, Claude or OpenAI costs $10k–$50k+ one-time. [Agency blog] — [Lets Viz](https://lets-viz.com/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-what-buyers-pay)
- On Fiverr the average agent build is ~$350. Budget gigs cost $90–$200, mid-range gigs $450–$750, and "advanced" gigs from $1,000. [Marketplace] — [Fiverr AI agents](https://block.fiverr.com/categories/programming-tech/ai-coding/ai-agents-development)
- One operator closed five clients at $900–$1,500 each, for $6,300 in a week. [Anecdotal] — [Substack](https://reviewaijournal.substack.com/p/the-6300-week-how-an-ai-app-builder)

**AI voice receptionists (Vapi / Retell / Synthflow etc.)**
- Typical agency pricing is a $500–$3,000 one-time setup fee plus a $300–$1,500/mo retainer. Custom flows, CRM integration and monitoring cost $497–$2,000/mo. White-label voice AI is priced at $297–$997/mo per client, with a claimed 50–70% margin. [Voice-platform vendor blogs, via search summary] — [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/voice-agent-pricing-strategy-guide); [Cekura](https://www.cekura.ai/blogs/how-to-price-ai-voice-agents); [Convocore/Medium](https://medium.com/convocore/how-to-price-ai-voice-agent-services-real-agency-models-a233aa1a849d)
- Agency retainers often bundle 500–2,000 minutes, with overage at $0.05–$0.15/min. [Vendor blog] — same sources as above
- Fiverr voice-agent setup gigs cost roughly $15–$500+. [Marketplace] — [Fiverr voice-agent gigs](https://block.fiverr.com/gigs/ai-voice-agent)

**Lead-gen / outreach and document processing**
- AI appointment-setter services cost $2,000–$8,000/mo on retainer. Done-for-you setters at $2k–$5k/mo book 80–120 meetings, which works out to ~$25–$60 per meeting. [Vendor blogs, via search summary] — [Myna](https://myna.cx/blog/ai-appointment-setter-cost/); [Solvea](https://solvea.cx/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-appointment-setter-cost)
- Document processing (OCR/IDP) builds: no specific agency price ranges were found (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- The same kind of build (for example, "a support chatbot") is quoted from ~$350 on Fiverr up to $75k at agencies. The price is set by the buyer segment, the accountability taken on, and the integration depth, not by the technology.
- For an SMB-focused new agency, a realistic menu is:
  - Single workflow: $1.5k–$5k.
  - Multi-workflow system: $5k–$15k.
  - Chat or RAG assistant: $3k–$15k.
  - Voice receptionist: $1k–$3k setup plus $300–$1,500/mo.
  - Custom agent: $7.5k–$25k.
  - Mid-market deals are 2–5x these figures.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of agency pricing exists. All ranges are self-reported by sellers.
- No sourced price ranges for document-processing / IDP builds or for lead-gen outreach builds as one-off projects.
- Reddit threads (r/automation, r/AI_Agents, r/n8n) could not be fetched, so community quotes are missing.

## 2. Hourly rates for automation freelancers/consultants

### Takeaway
Marketplace n8n, Make and Zapier freelancers mostly charge $25–$100/hr. Experienced AI-agent specialists charge $100–$150/hr. Vetted or senior consultants (Toptal, US agencies) charge $100–$300/hr. Most Upwork agencies cluster at $35–$49/hr.

### Cited Findings
- Freelance n8n experts typically charge $40–$100/hr. [Upwork-derived, via search summary; the Upwork page returned 403 to direct fetch] — [Upwork n8n experts](https://www.upwork.com/hire/n8n-experts/)
- Suggested n8n rate progression on Upwork:
  - $55–$75/hr when starting out.
  - $75–$95/hr after 5+ reviews and a Job Success Score above 90%.
  - $100–$150/hr for senior n8n and AI-agent specialists.
  - [Course/training blog] — [LearnForge](https://learnforge.dev/blog/sell-n8n-automation-upwork/)
- Senior specialists with Docker, AI and orchestration depth charge $125–$250+/hr. [Blog] — [AdSnipper](https://adsnipper.com/blog/hire-n8n-developer/)
- Some Contra n8n specialists charge $25–$50/hr, and most project-price rather than bill hourly. [Marketplace, via search summary] — [Contra](https://contra.com/hire/english-speaking-n8n-freelancers)
- Toptal clients typically pay $60–$150+/hr, and specialised talent costs above $200/hr. Toptal adds a $500 refundable deposit and a $79/mo subscription, and publishes no rate card. [Third-party review] — [South](https://www.hireinsouth.com/post/how-much-does-toptal-cost); [Toptal automation](https://www.toptal.com/developers/automation)
- Agency hourly rates run $100–$300 depending on region and seniority. [Agency blog] — [The Crunch](https://thecrunch.io/ai-automation-agency-cost/). Discovery, audit and architecture work runs $150–$250/hr. [Agency blog] — [Lets Viz](https://lets-viz.com/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-what-buyers-pay)
- Upwork bidding data from GigRadar (133,872 proposals, Dec 2025–Feb 2026):
  - The modal agency rate cohort is $35–$49/hr, with a 6.39% reply rate.
  - Undercutting the posted budget by more than 50% gets a 20.6% reply rate, and bidding at 200–500% of it gets 16.4%. Matching the budget gets 8.8%.
  - AI work grew 50% year over year, to ~$300M annualised, but the AI/ML category reply rate (7.21%) is below the platform mean.
  - [Verified dataset, but it measures reply rates, not market rates] — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)

### Inferences
- The middle of the rate range pays worst. Either compete on price, or position at a premium with outcome-based packaging.
- For a US-based cofounder team, $100–$175/hr is a defensible blended rate for scoping or overflow work. The main model should still be fixed-fee plus a monthly charge.

### Gaps
- The Zapier Experts, Make Partner and n8n Expert partner directories do not publish rates. No public rate data from them was found.
- Upwork's own rate pages returned 403, so the figures here are secondary.

## 3. Monthly retainer / maintenance / managed-automation pricing

### Takeaway
SMB retainers typically run $500–$3,500/mo. Mid-market retainers with ongoing builds run $2.5k–$10k/mo. Enterprise retainers run $8k–$25k/mo. The hybrid model (a fixed-fee first build, then a monthly retainer) is described as the 2026 default.

### Cited Findings
- By company size:
  - SMBs automating 2–3 workflows: $1,000–$3,500/mo.
  - Mid-market companies: $4,000–$10,000/mo.
  - Enterprise with dedicated teams: $8,000–$25,000/mo.
  - Median SMB/mid-market retainer: $2,800–$7,000/mo.
  - The hybrid model (fixed build plus retainer) is called the 2026 default.
  - [Agency blogs, via search summary] — [Taskip](https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-cost/); [Bowen AI](https://www.bowenaistrategygroup.com/ai-automation-agency-cost)
- Retainer tiers and what they include:

  | Tier | Price | Hours/mo | Included |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Starter | $2k–$4k/mo | 15–30 | Monitoring, incident response, 1 small workflow/mo |
  | Growth | $4k–$8k/mo | 40–70 | 2–3 new workflows/quarter, monthly eval reporting |
  | Scale | $8k–$15k/mo | 80–120 | Multi-department work, weekly stand-ups |

  Maintenance-only retainers cost $500–$5,000/mo. Hidden client costs on top: tool licences $50–$800/mo and LLM API spend $100–$5,000/mo. [Agency blog] — [The Crunch](https://thecrunch.io/ai-automation-agency-cost/)
- An AI automation retainer (monitoring, iteration, new launches) costs $2,500–$8,000/mo. The source frames a $4k/mo retainer as about half the ~$8.3k/mo fully-loaded cost of a senior automation developer. [Agency blog] — [Lets Viz](https://lets-viz.com/blogs/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-what-buyers-pay)
- Retainer tiers of $3k, $7.5k and $15k/mo. An "Agent License" model charges $20k setup plus $2k/mo, covering maintenance, API cost changes and model upgrades. [Agency blog] — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)
- The SMB sweet spot is companies with 30–150 employees, on 12-month engagements totalling $30k–$80k. [Agency blog] — [The Crunch](https://thecrunch.io/ai-automation-agency-cost/)

### Inferences
- Retainers should state an hours or workflow allowance, the monitoring and SLA terms, and who pays pass-through API and tool costs. Unclear pass-through terms are a common margin leak.

### Gaps
- No independent data on churn or average retainer length for AI automation agencies.

## 4. Value/performance-based pricing, setup+MRR models, white-label SaaS (GoHighLevel)

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern in the AAA community is setup plus MRR: $500–$3k setup and $297–$1,500/mo for SMB voice and chat agents. Performance pricing exists mainly in appointment setting. Rates of $5–$150 per booked appointment are cited, and the higher end fits high-ticket local verticals. GoHighLevel's $97–$497/mo agency plans are the standard white-label base.

### Cited Findings
- Pay-per-booked-appointment ranges:
  - One source cites $30–$150 per booked appointment, as an alternative to $2k–$8k/mo retainers.
  - Another cites $5–$25 per appointment for pure AI tools and calls it rare, because the provider absorbs the cost of non-converting calls.
  - The best fits are home services (solar, roofing, HVAC), real estate, med-spa, dental, and mortgage or insurance.
  - [Vendor blogs, via search summary; the ranges conflict] — [TaskBlink](https://taskblink.com/blog/appointment-setting-cost); [CallSetter](https://callsetter.ai/blog/ai-appointment-setting-cost); [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/ai-appointment-setter-explained)
- An AI appointment setter is claimed to cost ~$83 per booked appointment compared with a human setter. [Anecdotal, LinkedIn post] — [LinkedIn/Damon Torrey](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/damondtorrey_a-full-time-ai-appointment-setter-runs-about-activity-7470581919773077504-LIEw)
- Value-based framing: "$5,000 per implemented workflow that saves 10+ hours per week." [Agency blog] — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026)
- AI-agency gross margins are "frequently cited" at 70–90%, compared with 30–50% for social-media agencies. [Blog, unattributed claim] — [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-ai-agency-market-saturated-reddit)
- **GoHighLevel** [Verified-vendor] — [HighLevel pricing](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing):
  - Starter: $97/mo, with 3 sub-accounts.
  - Unlimited: $297/mo, with unlimited sub-accounts.
  - Agency Pro: $497/mo, which adds SaaS Mode (white-label subscriptions, automatic sub-account creation, and phone/email rebilling with markup).
  - Enterprise: custom pricing.
  - The "AI Employee" add-on costs $50/mo (Growth) or $97/mo (Unlimited) per sub-account. Telecom and AI usage is billed on top.

### Inferences
- A common SMB unit-economics template:
  - GHL Agency Pro at $497/mo spread across clients.
  - Plus a $97/mo AI Employee per sub-account, or voice minutes at ~$0.10–$0.15/min.
  - Resold at $297–$997/mo per client.
- Performance pricing only works when the agency controls lead flow and tracking. Otherwise disputes over attribution are likely.

### Gaps
- No verified data on percent-of-savings pricing, or on how many agencies use it.
- Community (Reddit) setup+MRR anecdotes could not be retrieved first-hand.

## 5. Competing productized AI tools (price ceilings)

### Takeaway
Off-the-shelf tools cap what agencies can charge for commodity builds:
- Support bots at ~$1 per resolution.
- AI receptionists at $20–$300/mo for SMBs.
- Raw voice infrastructure at ~$0.10–$0.15/min.

Agencies have to justify their premium through integration, customisation and done-for-you management.

### Cited Findings
- **Intercom Fin:** $0.99 per outcome (a resolution or procedure handoff). A qualification costs $9.99. Use with non-Intercom helpdesks has a 50-outcome/month minimum. Intercom seats start at $29/seat/mo. The Pro add-on starts at $99/mo, and Copilot costs $35/user/mo. Fin Voice pricing is custom. [Verified-vendor] — [fin.ai/pricing](https://fin.ai/pricing)
- **Retell AI:**
  - Components: $0.055/min voice infrastructure; TTS $0.015–$0.040/min; LLM $0.0016–$0.32/min; US telephony ~$0.015/min.
  - A typical configuration comes to ~$0.11/min, and the pay-as-you-go range is $0.07–$0.31/min.
  - No platform fee. 20 concurrent calls are free, then $8 per additional concurrent call per month.
  - [Verified-vendor] — [Retell pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)
- **Vapi:**
  - $0.05/min hosting fee. Model and voice costs are passed through at cost.
  - Plans: Core $29/mo; Pro at a $999/mo minimum; Premier custom.
  - All-in cost for ~1,000 min/mo is $82–$129.
  - [Verified-vendor] — [Vapi pricing](https://vapi.ai/pricing)
- **Smith.ai:**
  - Human virtual-receptionist plans: $292.50/mo for 30 calls, $765 for 90 calls, $1,950 for 300 calls. That is about $9.75/call, with $9.75–$10.50 overage.
  - AI receptionist: ~$1–$2/call. Hybrid: ~$2–$5/call.
  - [Third-party summaries, via search] — [Loman](https://loman.ai/blog/smith-ai-pricing); [CloudTalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/smith-ai-pricing/); primary: [smith.ai/pricing](https://smith.ai/pricing/receptionists)
- **My AI Front Desk:** Basic $20/mo ($16 annual); Business-in-a-Box $99/mo ($79 annual); voice beyond included minutes ~$0.25/min. [Third-party] — [CloudTalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/my-ai-front-desk-pricing/)
- **Goodcall:** ~$79/mo with unlimited minutes. [Third-party] — [Contractor ToolStack](https://contractortoolstack.com/compare/goodcall-vs-my-ai-front-desk/)
- AI receptionist SaaS ranges from $25 to $899/mo. [Vendor blog] — [AgentZap](https://agentzap.ai/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-complete-cost-guide-2025)

### Inferences
- **Voice cost versus charge:** 1,000 min/mo on Retell or Vapi costs about $100–$130. An agency charging $500–$1,500/mo has a large gross margin, but a client can buy My AI Front Desk or Goodcall for $79–$99. Agencies need to sell deep CRM, booking and industry integration, plus results guarantees.
- **Support-bot ceiling:** Fin at $0.99/resolution sets the ceiling for support deflection. A client with 1,000 resolutions/mo pays ~$1k/mo to Intercom. The agency's value has to come from content and knowledge-base prep, integrations, and multi-channel workflows.

### Gaps
- Synthflow and Bland current pricing pages were not fetched.
- Smith.ai AI-only plan prices were not verified on the primary page.

## 6. SMB vs mid-market, deal sizes, price compression

### Takeaway
- **SMB deals:** $1k–$15k builds and $300–$3.5k/mo, with 12-month value of ~$5k–$40k.
- **Mid-market deals:** $15k–$100k builds and $4k–$15k/mo, with 12-month value of $30k–$250k.
- **Price compression:** evidence exists at the commodity end (Fiverr, Upwork, sub-$100 SaaS receptionists), but none was found for specialised, vertical agencies.

### Cited Findings
- The SMB sweet spot is companies with 30–150 employees, on 12-month engagements totalling $30k–$80k. [Agency blog] — [The Crunch](https://thecrunch.io/ai-automation-agency-cost/)
- By segment [Agency blogs] — [Taskip](https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-cost/); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agency-services-pricing-strategies-2026):
  - Mid-market retainers run $4k–$10k/mo and enterprise retainers $8k–$25k/mo.
  - Premium agencies charge 2–3x the mid ranges.
  - New agencies may price at 60–80% of the mid ranges to build case studies.
- Signs of compression [Marketplace/blog]:
  - Fiverr "complete workflow" listings cost $45–$520.
  - Freelance n8n work is billed at ~$40–$100/hr, and first-year revenue is "usually well below what course marketing implies."
  - The "generalist AI agency doing n8n work for anyone" category is described as saturated. Specialists are said not to be.
  - Sources: [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/is-an-ai-automation-agency-worth-it); [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-ai-agency-market-saturated-reddit)
- AI-SDR segment market size: ~$4.39B growing toward $5.81B in 2026. [Blog, secondary claim] — [Ciela AI](https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-ai-agency-market-saturated-reddit)
- The Upwork AI/ML category has below-average reply rates (7.21% against a 7.45% mean) despite 50% year-over-year growth in AI work. That points to crowded supply. [Verified dataset] — [GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)
- Agencies are "scrambling to reprice AI-assisted work" in 2026, moving from selling time to selling results. [Blog] — [Kampaign Lab](https://www.kampaignlab.com/agency-freelance/how-agencies-are-pricing-ai-assisted-work-in-2026)
